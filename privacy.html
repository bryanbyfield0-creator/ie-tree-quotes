Privacy policy

Last updated 2026-10-01.

What we collect: the information you enter in our form (name, phone, email, city, and job details). Our form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which emails it to us. Our host (GitHub Pages) may log basic technical data such as IP addresses.

How we use it: only to respond to your request and to share it with independent tree service providers in your area so they can contact you about your job. We don't sell your information to data brokers and we don't use it for unrelated marketing.

Your choices: email bryanbyfield0@gmail.com to ask us to delete your information or to stop sharing it. California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA.

Contact: bryanbyfield0@gmail.com

Inland Empire Tree Quotes is a free referral service. We are not a tree service company, we don't do tree work, and we don't hold a contractor's license. When you send a request, we pass it to independent local tree service providers who can contact you with quotes. Any provider you hire is solely responsible for its work, licensing, and insurance. Always check a contractor's license at cslb.ca.gov and ask for proof of insurance before hiring.
