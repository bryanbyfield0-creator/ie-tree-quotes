Common tree jobs in Highland

  • Tree Removal: Removing dead, dying, leaning, or unwanted trees, including large trees close to houses, fences, and power lines. Pros usually take the tree down in sections, haul the wood away, and can grind the stump if you want.
  • Tree Trimming & Pruning: Crown thinning, raising the canopy, removing dead wood, and clearing branches away from roofs and walls. Good pruning reduces wind damage and keeps trees healthy.
  • Palm Tree Trimming: Removing dead fronds, seed pods, and skirts from fan palms, queen palms, date palms, and Mexican fan palms. Dead fronds can fall in wind and can be a fire hazard, so many owners trim palms once a year.
  • Stump Grinding: Grinding stumps below grade so you can replant, lay sod, or pave. It's usually much cheaper than full stump excavation.
  • Emergency & Storm Cleanup: Fallen trees and broken limbs after Santa Ana winds or storms, including trees on roofs, cars, or fences. Many tree companies offer same-day or next-day emergency service.
  • Brush Clearance & Defensible Space: Clearing dry brush, dead trees, and overgrowth to create defensible space around homes near the foothills and open land, and handling weed abatement notices.

Before you hire in Highland

  • Ask whether your job needs a city permit (some cities regulate street trees or certain species). The City of Highland or your HOA can tell you.
  • Check the contractor's license at cslb.ca.gov and get proof of insurance.
  • Get the scope in writing: haul-away, stump grinding, and cleanup.
  • For trees touching power lines, contact your electric utility first.
