Tree services in the Inland Empire

Here are the most common tree jobs homeowners and property managers ask about. Send one request and local providers can quote your job.

Tree Removal

Removing dead, dying, leaning, or unwanted trees, including large trees close to houses, fences, and power lines. Pros usually take the tree down in sections, haul the wood away, and can grind the stump if you want.

Get tree removal quotes

Tree Trimming & Pruning

Crown thinning, raising the canopy, removing dead wood, and clearing branches away from roofs and walls. Good pruning reduces wind damage and keeps trees healthy.

Get tree trimming & pruning quotes

Palm Tree Trimming

Removing dead fronds, seed pods, and skirts from Mexican fan palms, California fan palms, queen palms, and date palms. Dead fronds can fall in wind and can be a fire hazard, so many owners trim palms once a year.

Get palm tree trimming quotes

Stump Grinding

Grinding stumps below grade so you can replant, lay sod, or pave. It's usually much cheaper than full stump excavation.

Get stump grinding quotes

Emergency & Storm Cleanup

Fallen trees and broken limbs after Santa Ana winds or storms, including trees on roofs, cars, or fences. Many tree companies offer same-day or next-day emergency service.

Get emergency & storm cleanup quotes

Brush Clearance & Defensible Space

Clearing dry brush, dead trees, and overgrowth to create defensible space around homes near the foothills and open land, and handling weed abatement notices.

Get brush clearance & defensible space quotes

Prices depend on tree size, species, access, and hazards. The only reliable number is a written quote from a licensed, insured provider who has seen the job.
